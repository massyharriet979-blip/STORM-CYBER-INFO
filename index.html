STORM CYBER MD is a WhatsApp bot built by STORM X, a self-taught developer from Kasese, Uganda. It was built to protect WhatsApp groups and keep them clean, safe, and welcoming. WhatsApp is where people live, where friends talk, families share news, and communities grow. But groups were full of noise and unwanted messages. Admins were tired, members were leaving, and good groups were dying.

STORM X started coding on September 28th, 2026, with only a phone, no computer, no degree, and only a dream. He built this bot to help admins, to give groups peace, and to make members feel safe again. It works day and night, like a guard who never sleeps, so people can talk, laugh, and grow together.

It is free of charge for everyone. No payment, no hidden cost. Just help from a young boy with a big heart who believes young people from Uganda can change the world. This is only the beginning. By 2030, STORM X will be the best. This is his storm.
